# BigCommerce to Shopify

Copy this file into your tracker and tick items per phase. Each line is one check. "Why it bites" is the failure that shows up after launch if you skip it. These are the BigCommerce checks, written so this file can be used on its own. The WooCommerce list is [CHECKLIST.md](CHECKLIST.md).

Practise on [test-catalogs/bigcommerce-basic](test-catalogs/bigcommerce-basic). `bigcommerce.csv` is a fictional legacy Bulk Edit export. `shopify.csv` is that catalog in the Shopify product CSV. The mapping used in the pair is in [test-catalogs/README.md](test-catalogs/README.md).

## Before you start

- [ ] Freeze product edits, then export from the experience the store still has. Why it bites: the legacy tool can only be used in a store with a single native storefront. The modern tool can be used in a single-storefront store and in a Multi-Storefront store, and "it is a prerequisite for enabling Multi-Storefront." "You cannot revert back to the legacy Import/Export experience after enabling Multi-Storefront," though a banner before that step "provides the option to Rollback to the previous version." [Legacy overview](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-Import-Export-Overview)
- [ ] On the legacy experience, choose Bulk Edit when you want the majority of fields, or Default when you only need the basics. Why it bites: Bulk Edit "was made for updating your product information" and "includes the majority of exportable fields and has specifically named columns that let you skip steps in the importing process." Default "includes only basic product details such as name, weight, description, and price." Custom templates are created at Settings › Export templates, and BigCommerce recommends a copy of Bulk Edit for editing products in bulk. [Legacy export](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Exporting-Products)
- [ ] On the modern experience, take the default product CSV. Why it bites: "All exportable fields are included in the default product CSV file; custom export templates are not supported," and the modern overview says the same without naming a Bulk Edit template. The sale-pricing page recommends a custom export template for "a large number of products without options" or regular sale-price changes. It does not say which tool, and only the legacy tool has custom templates, so check which tool your store has. [Modern export](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Exporting-Your-Products) [Modern overview](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Modern-Import-Export-Overview) [Sale pricing](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Using-Sale-Pricing)
- [ ] On a legacy export, copy Bulk Edit at Settings › Export templates, check Include Product Options in the copy, then export. Why it bites: "If you want variant options and modifier rules to be present under products in your CSV file, check the box next to Include Product Options." That box is a custom export template setting, and BigCommerce does not say whether the built-in Bulk Edit template includes options. "The legacy Import/Export tool uses Y or N Boolean values," and Boolean Format can change them, so check that SKU and Rule rows are in the file. [Legacy export](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Exporting-Products)

## Product data

Note on the two row shapes: a legacy SKU row "includes variant identifiers, stock levels, and dimensions," and a Rule row "defines the price, weight, and image that appears when the variant is selected on the storefront." The modern column says "All variant information is present in a single Variant row." The transfer page, on a custom export template, says that apart from the variant image URL, "all information in Rule rows are also present in the SKU rows," which is not the legacy table's split. Check in your own export which cells are filled on which row. [Legacy overview](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-Import-Export-Overview) [Legacy to modern transfer](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-to-Modern-Product-Transfer)

Note on option text: the complex-attributes, first-import, and updating pages all write two options on one variant as `[RB]Size=Small,[CS]Color=Blue:#0000FF`, with no space before or after the comma. Check in your own export. [Complex attributes](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Complex-Attributes) [Importing your first product](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Importing-Your-First-Product) [Updating products](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Updating-via-Import)

Note on sale price and retail price: sale price and MSRP "cannot be assigned to variants via import," but the modern column says "Adding and updating Retail (MSRP) Price and Sale Price is supported on variants." The legacy tool updates Sale Price "for products without options or variants." The FAQ, answering a question about the legacy (v2) product experience, says "Product option price rules that are Fixed are not affected by Sale Price." [Legacy fields](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-Product-Fields) [Legacy overview](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-Import-Export-Overview) [Sale pricing](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Using-Sale-Pricing)

Note on a blank price: a new product says "If left blank, the product will have a price of 0." A new modern variant says "Leave the Price field blank if you want the variant to have the same price as the base product." The transfer template says "If a variant’s price or weight is the same as the base product, those fields will be blank in the Variant row." That describes the template, not a Bulk Edit file. [First import](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Importing-Your-First-Product) [Modern complex attributes](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Importing-Complex-Attributes) [Legacy to modern transfer](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-to-Modern-Product-Transfer)

Note on names that do not match: the fields page says "This field is called Product Image File - 1 in the Bulk Edit template" for `Product Image Path - 1`, yet its Manually Entered Fields table also lists `Product Image File - 1` as the WebDAV location of the image. The digital file there is `Product File Path- 1`, with no space before the hyphen, while the complex-attributes page names `Product File Path - 2`. Modern images ask for `Item`, `ID`, and `Image URL (Import)`, and a later table says `Item Type` and `Product ID`. [Legacy fields](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-Product-Fields) [Complex attributes](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Complex-Attributes) [Modern complex attributes](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Importing-Complex-Attributes)

- [ ] Read Item Type before you count rows as products. Why it bites: "Type in Product for products, SKU for variants, and Rule for variant or modifier rules." On the modern file the column is Item, and "adding “variant” will cause import errors" if the casing is wrong. The transfer template renames Item Type to Item, Product ID to ID, and Product Name to Name. [Legacy fields](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-Product-Fields) [Modern complex attributes](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Importing-Complex-Attributes) [Legacy to modern transfer](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-to-Modern-Product-Transfer)
- [ ] When a product has SKU rows, map each SKU row to one Shopify variant under the product's handle, and leave the Product row's SKU out of the variant list. A product with no SKU rows is one variant and keeps the Product row's SKU. Why it bites: with SKU rows present, the parent SKU is the product and each SKU row is a variant, which BigCommerce calls "SKU for variants." "Do not use commas in SKUs," a SKU is case-insensitive with a maximum of 255 characters, and Product ID is "The system-generated product identifier." [Legacy fields](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-Product-Fields)
- [ ] Read the option text on the SKU row's Product Name. Why it bites: a size option is written `[RB]Size=Small`. Two options on one variant are separated by a comma with no space before or after, as in the note above, for example `[RB]Size=Small,[CS]Color=Blue:#0000FF`. On an existing SKU row, Product Name is read-only, and changing an option name creates a new option. [Complex attributes](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Complex-Attributes) [Updating products](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Updating-via-Import)
- [ ] Take the variant price from the SKU row's Price, then read the Rule row before you drop it. Why it bites: "Variant weight and price are displayed in the SKU row, but must be updated in the Rule row." A rule price is `[ADD]#`, `[REMOVE]#`, or `[FIXED]#`, for example `[FIXED]25` or `[ADD]25%`, so a tag is not a Shopify Price. Updating an existing rule requires the ID, so check in your own export. [Updating products](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Updating-via-Import) [Complex attributes](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Complex-Attributes)
- [ ] Do not write Sale Price or Retail Price onto a variant from a legacy import column. Why it bites: "Sale price cannot be assigned to variants via import" and "MSRP cannot be assigned to variants via import." "if entered, it will replace the base price" is what Sale Price does on the product, and "if entered, it will appear next to your base or sale price" is what Retail Price does. When you are creating options, "Adding sale or retail (MSRP) pricing" is not supported, and the rest of the disagreement is in the note above. [Legacy fields](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-Product-Fields) [First import](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Importing-Your-First-Product)
- [ ] Read Sale Price and Retail Price before you copy either number. Why it bites: "When Sale Price is set on a product, that value will override the Default Price on the storefront." "Adding a MSRP (Retail Price) will show it with a strikethrough, and will display the difference (amount saved) between the retail price and regular price (or Sale Price if entered)." [Sale pricing](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Using-Sale-Pricing)
- [ ] Put the amount the customer pays in Shopify Price. Put Retail Price in Compare-at price only when you want that struck-through display. Why it bites: "Make sure that the value in the Compare-at price field is higher than the value in the Price field to display the sale price." "only the sale price is displayed at checkout." Filling Compare-at from Retail Price can make the theme show a sale even when Sale Price is empty, because the theme controls whether and where a sale is shown. [Shopify sale prices](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/details/product-pricing/sale-pricing)
- [ ] Leave a blank price blank until you know which of the three statements applies. Why it bites: a blank becoming 0 is what BigCommerce documents for creating a new product, and a blank that means the same price as the base product is the modern create-a-variant case and, separately, the custom transfer template. The Shopify Price column says "The default value is 0.00 if no value is entered," which is the column, not a BigCommerce rule. The three statements are in the note above. [Product CSV columns](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/import-export/using-csv) [First import](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Importing-Your-First-Product)
- [ ] On a legacy import of existing products, leave Ignore blank values checked unless a blank cell should delete data. Why it bites: "Unchecking this setting will delete the data associated with any blank field in your import file." The modern overview does not support specifying "Ignore blank values." That setting is not the create-a-product rule that a blank price becomes 0. [Updating products](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Updating-via-Import) [Modern overview](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Modern-Import-Export-Overview)
- [ ] Keep a Rule row that has no variant SKU as a modifier adjustment, not a variant. Why it bites: "you can only adjust the modifier’s price and weight using the [ADD] or [REMOVE] adjustment tags; setting a fixed amount is not supported." The legacy overview says you can "add or edit price and weight rules for existing modifier options," but the transfer page says "product modifier options cannot be imported or exported in either experience," and Rule rows with no SKU "cannot be imported using the modern Import/Export tool." [Updating products](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Updating-via-Import) [Legacy overview](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-Import-Export-Overview) [Legacy to modern transfer](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-to-Modern-Product-Transfer)
- [ ] Delete Option Set and Option Set Align before a v3 import, and do not look for them in the Shopify file. Why it bites: "Keeping the Option Set and Option Set Align fields in your CSV file can cause duplicated variants upon import." The Shopify product CSV has no column of that name. [Updating products](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Updating-via-Import) [Product CSV columns](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/import-export/using-csv)
- [ ] Do not look for bulk pricing in the product CSV. Why it bites: "If a field is not in the list below (such as bulk pricing), it cannot be imported, exported, or created manually in the CSV file." The FAQ answers "Can I assign bulk pricing to products using a CSV import file?" with "No." "You can have up to 50 rules for each product," "Variant-level bulk pricing is possible using Price Lists," and the product-page price "does not dynamically adjust based on the currently selected quantity." [Legacy fields](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-Product-Fields) [Bulk pricing](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Bulk-Pricing)
- [ ] Use the Price List Importer/Exporter app to bulk edit existing price lists or create new ones, not the product CSV. Why it bites: "Price lists let you offer advanced variant-level price overrides based on storefronts and customer groups." "any customer group-storefront combination can only be assigned to a single price list at a time," and "You cannot use both customer group discounts and a price list at the same time for a customer group." Price lists need a Performance plan, and the storefront cannot use a Blueprint theme. [Price lists](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Price-Lists)
- [ ] Read a missing price-list row as catalog price, not as a zero. Why it bites: "If an active Price List does not contain prices for a variant, the catalog pricing will be used," and on a multi-currency store a missing price falls back to the catalog price "or in the case of multi-currency, auto-converted prices." "If a variant has a Price Record, any existing product-level bulk pricing will not apply to the cart." "For variants without Price Records, any existing product bulk pricing will apply." [Price list API](https://developer.bigcommerce.com/docs/rest-management/price-lists)
- [ ] Keep price lists, customer groups, and quantity breaks out of Shopify Price. Why it bites: the main columns are Price and Compare-at price. Some markets also add Price / [Market] and Compare At Price / [Market], which are not BigCommerce price lists, and a market has those columns only if "exactly one of its active catalogs isn't shared with another market." [Product CSV columns](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/import-export/using-csv)
- [ ] Decide which storefront is the Shopify store before you copy a name or a price. Why it bites: "Storefronts share your store’s catalog. You can choose which products appear on which storefronts." "product attributes (like description and name) cannot be overridden into a different language without customization," and you can "Use Price Lists for custom pricing on each storefront" and "Control product purchasability per storefront." [Multi-storefront](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Multi-Storefront)
- [ ] Read the modern Channels cell as storefront ids, and do not expect that cell on Shopify. Why it bites: "Your default storefront will always have a channel ID of 1," and further ids are separated by a semicolon. Shopify's product CSV has no storefront column. `Published on online store` is the online store channel, and "CSV files can't be used to bulk update product availability on other sales channels." [Modern fields](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Supported-Product-Fields) [Product CSV columns](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/import-export/using-csv)
- [ ] Map Product Visible? separately from Allow Purchases?, and do not read Product Availability as a status. Why it bites: Product Visible? uses Y to be visible and N to "disable product visibility," while Allow Purchases? uses Y to allow purchase or pre-order and N when the product "cannot be purchased or pre-ordered." Product Availability is "A brief description of how long it takes for your product to ship." Product Not Visible is read-only, so use Product Visible?. [Legacy fields](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-Product-Fields)
- [ ] Set Shopify Status and Published on online store on purpose. Why it bites: the CSV values are `active`, `draft`, and `archived`, and `true` or `false` for the online store. If the Status column is absent, "the product status is automatically uploaded as active." The admin also has Unlisted, "accessible only by its URL," and that word is not in this CSV value list. [Product CSV columns](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/import-export/using-csv) [Product status](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/details/product-details-page)
- [ ] Copy Product URL into the handle without its leading and trailing slash, and choose the handle yourself if the path has other characters or more than one segment. Why it bites: the format is `/new-relative-url/`, up to 1,024 characters. The fields page says a blank URL "will default to the product name," and the first-import page says it is "automatically generated using the selected format in your URL structure settings," so check in your own export. A valid URL handle "can contain letters, dashes, and numbers, but it can't contain any spaces." [Legacy fields](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-Product-Fields) [First import](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Importing-Your-First-Product) [Product CSV columns](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/import-export/using-csv)
- [ ] Give each product its own handle. Why it bites: the URL handle is "The unique identifier for each product" and "is used in the URL of the product page." [Product CSV columns](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/import-export/using-csv)
- [ ] Give every image a public https URL in Shopify's `Product image URL`, one image per row. Why it bites: "Shopify downloads the images during the import," and the file name doesn't include `_thumb`, `_small`, or `_medium` suffixes. [Product CSV columns](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/import-export/using-csv)
- [ ] Check which Bulk Edit image cell holds a URL before you copy it. Why it bites: `Product Image File - 1` is "the image's WebDAV file path" on the updating page, while the fields page says the Path column holds "file name (if stored in WebDAV) or full URL." `Product Image URL - 1`, "The image's full URL on your storefront," is read-only and is not in Bulk Edit. [Updating products](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Updating-via-Import) [Legacy fields](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-Product-Fields)
- [ ] Copy Product Image Description into Image alt text. Why it bites: Product Image Description is "The alt tag" with a maximum of 255 characters, and Shopify's image alt text "the maximum length is 512 characters," so it fits. [Legacy fields](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-Product-Fields) [Product CSV columns](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/import-export/using-csv)
- [ ] Count images per product. Why it bites: a base product can have a maximum of 1,000 images in BigCommerce, and Shopify allows up to 250 images on a product. [Updating products](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Updating-via-Import) [Product CSV columns](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/import-export/using-csv)
- [ ] Put a variant image on that variant, one file only. Why it bites: "Only one image can be added per variant." On a legacy rule the column is `Product Image File - 1`, and on the modern file it is Variant Image URL, on the Variant row, and "image descriptions are not supported on variant images." Supported types are JPEG, JPG, GIF, WebP, and PNG, and the maximum size is 8 MB. [Updating products](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Updating-via-Import) [Modern complex attributes](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Importing-Complex-Attributes)
- [ ] Read Track Inventory before you copy Current Stock Level. Why it bites: the values are `none`, `by product`, and `by option` (the modern file uses `product` and `variant`), and the page says `by option` is "to track inventory by variant." The fields page joins `by product` and `by option` with a non-breaking space, so check the exact characters and which row holds each variant's quantity in your own export. The Shopify quantity column "applies only to Shopify stores that have a single location." [Legacy fields](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-Product-Fields) [Modern fields](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Supported-Product-Fields) [Product CSV columns](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/import-export/using-csv)
- [ ] Convert weight to whole grams after you check which unit the cell uses. Why it bites: Product Weight says "Use numbers only; no units." When you are creating a new product, units "are automatically applied by your store’s physical dimension settings," and a blank weight "will have a weight of 0," so check in your own export. Shopify wants "only the numerical value, without the unit of measurement or decimals," so 5.125 kilograms is `5125` and 4 pounds is `1814`, and display units are `g`, `kg`, `lb`, and `oz`. [Legacy fields](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-Product-Fields) [First import](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Importing-Your-First-Product) [Product CSV columns](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/import-export/using-csv)
- [ ] Stay within three Shopify options, and count variants against both caps. Why it bites: "A single product can have a maximum of 600 SKUs/variants. If your product has more than 600 variations, we recommend using a modifier option." "You can create up to 2,048 variants for a product" and "Each product can have up to three options," though some themes and apps might not support more than 100. [Product options](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Product-Options-v3) [Adding variants](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/variants/add-variants)
- [ ] Read categories as names on a legacy file and as ids on a modern file. Why it bites: legacy uses category names and parent/child paths, and it can create categories, while modern uses category ids and cannot create categories. The fields-page example is `Category1; Category2/Subcategory1;Category2/Subcategory2`, and the complex-attributes page says to separate categories with a semicolon "without a space." A product can have 1,000 categories, the store 16,000, and a category name 50 characters, so check in your own export. [Legacy fields](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-Product-Fields) [Complex attributes](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Complex-Attributes) [Modern overview](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Modern-Import-Export-Overview)
- [ ] Put one collection name in Shopify's Collection column. Why it bites: that column is import-only, is not in the export, accepts one collection, and the maximum length is 255 characters. [Product CSV columns](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/import-export/using-csv)
- [ ] Send custom fields that are not options to product metafields, and check which character limit you exported. Why it bites: a legacy cell is `name=value` pairs separated by semicolons, with a maximum of 255 characters per name or value and 200 fields, while a modern cell is JSON, still 200 fields, and "Custom field names and values each have a maximum character limit of 250," so those character limits disagree. "Variant metafields aren't supported" in the Shopify product CSV. Product metafields are, after a definition exists. [Legacy fields](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-Product-Fields) [Modern complex attributes](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Importing-Complex-Attributes) [Product CSV columns](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/import-export/using-csv)
- [ ] Do not look for bulk pricing, price lists, customer groups, storefronts, or modifier rules in the Shopify product CSV. Why it bites: the column table has none of those columns. The transfer page lists digital files, product filters, product reviews, and brand and category data as attributes the modern BigCommerce import tool cannot import, which is a BigCommerce limit, not a Shopify one. [Product CSV columns](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/import-export/using-csv) [Legacy to modern transfer](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-to-Modern-Product-Transfer)
- [ ] Decide yourself whether Brand Name goes to Shopify's Vendor. Why it bites: Vendor is "The name of the origin or source of the products that you sell," Brand Name is "The brand or manufacturer of a product," and neither page maps one to the other. [Product CSV columns](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/import-export/using-csv) [Legacy fields](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-Product-Fields)
- [ ] Copy a filled Page Title and Meta Description into SEO title and SEO description, and do not invent a keywords column. Why it bites: Page Title "will default to the product name if left blank," and Meta Description "will default to what's entered in your homepage SEO fields" with a maximum of 65,535 characters. The Shopify file has SEO title (up to 70 characters) and SEO description (up to 320), no meta-keywords column, and left blank it uses the title and the description. [Legacy fields](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-Product-Fields) [Product CSV columns](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/import-export/using-csv)

## Customers and orders

- [ ] Import products first, then customers, then historical orders. Why it bites: orders need products and customers already in the store if those records are to connect. [Migrate to Shopify](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/migrating-to-shopify)
- [ ] Export customers from Customers › Export, not from the product file. Why it bites: Default "Includes only basic customer details, such as first name, last name, email address, phone number, and addresses," and Bulk Edit "Includes the majority of exportable fields." A BigCommerce customer import requires Email Address, First Name, and Last Name, and is capped at 2 MB ("Customer import files must be 2 MB or less"), which matters only if you import a file back into BigCommerce. Shopify's limit is in the Shopify import item below. [Customer import and export](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Importing-Exporting-Customers)
- [ ] Do not expect a password in the customer export. Why it bites: "passwords cannot be exported via CSV file, however they can be imported" into BigCommerce, and the Orders count is "exportable only" and not in the Bulk Edit template by default. [Customer import and export](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Importing-Exporting-Customers)
- [ ] Use Shopify's province and country codes, not BigCommerce's spelled-out state. Why it bites: if you edit the file and import it back into BigCommerce, changing the email column "will create a new customer record" and the state "must be spelled out, not abbreviated." Those are BigCommerce rules. Shopify's customer CSV uses `Default Address Province Code` and `Default Address Country Code`, an ISO code and an ISO Alpha-2 code. [Customer import and export](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Importing-Exporting-Customers) [Importing customers](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/customers/import-export-customers)
- [ ] Import Shopify customers with the customer CSV, and plan a new password. Why it bites: "You can only import customer CSV files that are 15 MB or smaller." The Email column is required, and the value can be blank, and Total Spent and Total Orders "aren't imported." "you need to invite your customers to create new passwords," and with sign-in links customers receive a one-time 6-digit code and "A password isn't required to sign in." [Importing customers](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/customers/import-export-customers) [Customer accounts](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/customers/customer-accounts)
- [ ] Export orders from Orders › Export Orders. Move history into Shopify with an app or the Order API, not with a product CSV or a customer CSV. Why it bites: "importing orders can only be done via the API" is the BigCommerce import direction. Shopify's migration guide lists historical orders under migration apps, the Order API, and the Transaction API, and "You can't import order information using a customer CSV file." People set to receive new-order mail get one email per imported historical order unless those notifications are off. [Order import and export](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Importing-Exporting-Orders) [Migrate to Shopify](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/migrating-to-shopify) [Importing customers](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/customers/import-export-customers)

## Content

- [ ] Copy pages across, or move them with an app or the Page API. Why it bites: the product export is not a page export, and the migration guide does not offer a page CSV. [Migrate to Shopify](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/migrating-to-shopify)
- [ ] Copy blog posts into a Shopify blog, or move them with an app or the Blog API. Why it bites: posts are not in the product CSV, and Shopify's migration guide lists blogs under migration apps, the Blog API, and the Blog Article API. On Multi-Storefront, "some store functions are global and are shared across storefronts, such as the built-in blog," so the posts are one set for every storefront. [Multi-storefront](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Multi-Storefront) [Migrate to Shopify](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/migrating-to-shopify)
- [ ] Plan reviews on their own. Why it bites: the legacy field list has no reviews column, and a field that is not in it "cannot be imported, exported, or created manually in the CSV file." The transfer page also lists product reviews among the attributes the modern BigCommerce tool cannot import. Check in your own export for where reviews live before you assume they arrived with the catalog. [Legacy fields](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-Product-Fields) [Legacy to modern transfer](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-to-Modern-Product-Transfer)

## SEO

- [ ] Export BigCommerce redirects from Settings › 301 Redirect, using Export All, for one storefront or for all of them. Why it bites: the redirect CSV import file has five columns, Domain, Old Path, Manual URL/Path, Dynamic Target Type, and Dynamic Target ID, and the export option "Export dynamic redirect URLs" adds Dynamic Target URL (read-only). Dynamic Target Type can be Product, Brand, Category, Page, or Blog Post, and Manual URL/Path "will be blank" for a dynamic redirect. "301 redirects are automatically created whenever the name or the URL of a product, category, or webpage is changed." [301 redirects](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/301-Redirects)
- [ ] Set Redirect Old URL? only when you still need that redirect inside BigCommerce. Why it bites: "Type in Y to create a 301 redirect, or N to not create a redirect from the old product URL." A redirect created there stays in BigCommerce. The old path you still need on Shopify is a separate redirect. [Legacy fields](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-Product-Fields)
- [ ] Add a Shopify URL redirect for every old path you still need. Why it bites: "You can redirect only from broken URLs," and if the URL still loads a valid webpage, "the URL redirect won't work." You cannot redirect fixed paths such as `/products`, `/collections`, or `/collections/all`, or a path that begins with `/apps`, `/application`, `/cart`, `/carts`, `/orders`, `/services`, or `/shop`. The page also reserves `/collections/vendors`, `/collections/types`, `/a/`, `/community/`, and `/tools/`, and MyShopify domain paths ending in `/shop` or `/services`, and a store can store 100,000 URL redirects, or 20,000,000 on the Plus plan. [URL redirects](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/menus-and-links/url-redirect)
- [ ] Do not point a `.html` URL at the same path with `.html` removed. Why it bites: "URLs ending in .html cannot be redirected to the same URL without .html. Shopify treats these as the same URL." Query strings "might not work as expected," and BigCommerce's own reserved list (`/index.php`, `/admin`, `/manage`, `/graphql`, plus more on a Blueprint theme) limits redirects made inside BigCommerce, while Shopify's list is in the previous item. [URL redirects](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/menus-and-links/url-redirect) [301 redirects](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/301-Redirects)
- [ ] Read the new path from the live Shopify page before you write the redirect. Why it bites: BigCommerce describes Product URL as "The relative path of your product's URL on your store," and the Shopify page says only that the URL handle "is used in the URL of the product page," so read the full path from the live page. [Product CSV columns](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/import-export/using-csv) [Legacy fields](https://support.bigcommerce.com/s/article/Legacy-Product-Fields)
- [ ] After you remove a redirect, request the old URL again. Why it bites: "301 redirects are cached by browsers and search engines. If you remove a redirect after customers have accessed it, then the redirect might continue to work in their browsers until the cache expires." [URL redirects](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/menus-and-links/url-redirect)
