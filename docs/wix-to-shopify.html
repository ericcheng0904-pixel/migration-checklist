Wix to Shopify catalog checklist

A checklist for a small catalog moving from Wix Stores to Shopify. It follows the same phases as CHECKLIST.md. Wix documents two catalogs. This file covers the older catalog's CSV in full, because that is the format with an official template. For the new catalog it says only what Wix's pages settle, and it says to check in your own export for the rest.

Before you start

  • Work out which catalog the store is on before you choose an export. Why it bites: the introducing page's features are "available with the new Wix Stores product catalog", and a store that does not show them may not have been migrated yet. If the first field on the product creation page is Images and videos, the new-catalog CSV pages say you are on the previous version. Customers and orders

  • Export contacts from Contacts, not from the product file, and only as the site owner. Why it bites: "only the site owner can export contacts", up to 50,000 at a time, from Import / Export, as a Regular CSV, a Google CSV, or an Outlook CSV. Shopify's guide says to choose the regular CSV file format. Exporting contacts Migrate from Wix
  • Import customers only after the headings match Shopify's customer CSV. Why it bites: "You can't import a customer CSV that doesn't match the Shopify headings." That import has no data mapping support. The customer CSV page says passwords cannot be migrated with that file, and that Total Spent and Total Orders aren't imported. Migrate from Wix Importing customers
  • Export orders from the Orders tab, and keep the time zone and the row shape. Why it bites: the page is relevant for Wix Stores, and the time of purchase "is always given in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC)." Item purchased is one row per item, and Orders is one row per order. You can deselect columns. Order history is not in the product CSV. Shopify's guide names third-party migration apps for order history and describes no order CSV import. Content

  • Do not expect the product CSV to carry pages or reviews. Why it bites: Shopify's guide says "You can't export or migrate reviews from Wix to Shopify." The product mapping table has no review column and no page column. The guide's website step is about themes, not a page file. Migrate from Wix

SEO

  • Save the live product path, not the handleId, and add a Shopify redirect for each one. Why it bites: the default Wix path is /product-page/{product slug} and the Shopify path is /products/{url handle}. You can redirect only from broken URLs. You cannot redirect from prefixes such as /apps, /cart, /orders, or /shop, or from /products, /collections, and /collections/all. A store can have 100,000 redirects, or 20,000,000 on Plus. Launch day

  • Spot-check one surcharge, one PERCENT discount, one AMOUNT discount, and one visible value. Why it bites: a blank surcharge is not a discount, a percentage is not an amount, and visible is case sensitive. The Store Migration app adds products as drafts, which is a different import from the CSV path. On test-catalogs/wix-basic, Large is the surcharge, Reed Sample Ladle is PERCENT, and Cove Sample Cloth is AMOUNT. Migrate from Wix Importing products
  • Place the test orders the migration guide lists, then refund, cancel, fulfill, and archive. Why it bites: the guide says to place a successful and a failed transaction, refund and cancel an order, fulfill or partially fulfill an order, and archive a successful order, "to make sure that everything is working." Migrate from Wix

The first week

  • Recount variants, and recount the old product paths. Why it bites: on the Wix sheet, count fieldType Variant rows, and count a Product row only when it has no options and no Variant rows. A Product row with options but no Variant rows is one variant per option value. Do not count a Product row that has Variant rows, and do not count by sku. On the Shopify sheet, skip a row that repeats the handle only to add an image. Open each saved /product-page/ path and confirm the matching /products/ path. WIX.md URL redirects
