Squarespace to Shopify

Copy this file into your tracker and tick items per phase. Each line is one check. "Why it bites" is the failure that shows up after launch if you skip it. These are the Squarespace checks, written so this file can be used on its own. The WooCommerce list is CHECKLIST.md.

Practise on test-catalogs/squarespace-basic. squarespace.csv is a fictional product export. shopify.csv is that catalog in the Shopify product CSV. The mapping used in the pair is in test-catalogs/README.md.

Before you start

  • Freeze product edits, then export from the Products panel. Why it bites: open the Products & Services panel, click Products, then Export all, or tick the checkbox beside the featured image and click Export. "You can export up to 10,000 products." Later edits do not flow back into that file. Export products

Why it bites: the Export all steps are headed Export all physical and service products, and the selection steps carry no such heading. The download-products page says it is not possible "to export download products." The import page lists download products as not importable. Export products Download products Importing products

The inventory page says you can bulk edit "physical and service products" from a products .csv, and it does not mention gift cards. Whether a gift card row is exported is not said.   • Count columns against the example, then look in your own export for columns the example does not have. Note on version 7.0 and 7.1 in the import article: it allows "up to 10,000 products to a version 7.1 site," up to 200 to a version 7.0 site, and up to 250 variants per product in both versions. On a move between sites, Product Page is required on 7.1 and must match an existing store page. On 7.0 it is optional, and deleting the value creates a new, disabled, not-linked store page. The 7.0 bulk-edit table and the 7.0 import-from-another-platform table say the new page is not-linked, and they do not say disabled. Categories are required on the 7.1 tables and optional on the 7.0 tables. The 7.1 move table says products have a /p/ before the URL slug. The export article does not split its steps by version. Product data

Note on variant caps: the pages disagree and do not say which applies. The variants page (updated 22 October 2026) says each product can have "up to six options and 250 variant combinations" in the editor or the .csv importer. The import page says up to 250 variants per product. The store page types page (updated 17 July 2025) lists up to 100 variants per product for all stores. The import tables document three option pairs, not six. The 10,000 and 200 product figures agree between the import page and the store page types page. Customers and orders

  • Export the customer list you actually opened, and keep it apart from the product file. Why it bites: the Lists & Segments file lists Account, names, Email, order totals, last-order date, Customer Since, last-used addresses, Tags, and no password, and "Notes aren't included in the exported .csv file." Shopify's guide uses Contacts and Export all contacts, omits notes and saved addresses except the latest physical order's address, and names the Squarespace columns Accepts Marketing and Shipping Address 1, which are not in Squarespace's export column list, so check the headings in your download. SEO

Note on a moved product: the mappings page says https://mybusiness.com/shop1/item would become https://mybusiness.com/shop2/item, with no /p/, and the redirect lines on that page are /shop1/p/item -> /shop2/p/item 301. URL mappings

  • Save the Squarespace URL mappings you still need, then build those redirects again on Shopify. Why it bites: mappings live under Developer tools as /old-url -> /new-url 301, and the URL mappings field "has a limit of 400 KB, which is usually around 2500 redirect lines." A 301 needs the old URL gone and the new URL present, and a redirect works only after the old page is deleted or disabled. A 7.1 example is /store/p/[name] -> /buy/p/[name] 301, and the page says you can only redirect from domains connected to your Squarespace site. URL mappings URL slugs
  • Add a Shopify URL redirect for every old path you still need, and read the live path before you write it. Why it bites: the product CSV has no redirect column. The handle "is used in the URL of the product page," and on 7.1 the old path also has the store page and p/ before the slug. Shopify's redirects page says redirects work only from broken URLs and lists prefixes it cannot redirect, including /shop, so check a Squarespace store page slug against that list. Launch day

  • Spot-check one variant product, one On Sale No row whose Sale Price is filled, one Unlimited stock cell, and one Visible value. Why it bites: Shopify's guide says imported products marked as hidden "aren't published until you make them available to your sales channels," and that a product missing a variant option won't be imported successfully. On test-catalogs/squarespace-basic, JAR-S is on sale, JAR-L and LADLE-FERN keep the regular price, and CLOTH-MIST is the unlimited row. Migrate from Squarespace Importing products
  • Place test orders before you call checkout done. Why it bites: Shopify's Migrate from Squarespace guide lists a successful and a failed transaction, refunding and canceling an order, fulfilling or partially fulfilling an order, and archiving a successful order as test orders to run. It says to try them "to make sure that everything is working". Migrate from Squarespace
